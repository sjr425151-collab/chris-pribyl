# 00 — Project Charter and Executive Mandate

**Source:** Executive Directive 001 (AUTH-001), 2026-11-27. **Status:** Approved (directive content). Wording here condenses the directive. Where they differ, the directive governs.

## 1. Mandate

Engineer the flagship corporate website for Spotlight Marketing LLC. It is a foundational corporate digital asset, meant to:

- establish market authority,
- generate qualified business opportunities,
- communicate multidisciplinary expertise, and
- support the company's long-term expansion.

It is **not** a conventional agency site, a template build or a redesign of the existing website.

The ambition is to exceed the standards of the top 0.1% of professional digital experiences. That ambition must be expressed as **measurable, defensible, independently verifiable** standards ([03-quality-standards](03-quality-standards.md)), not as subjective claims. Quality, precision, strategic intelligence, maintainability and long-term business value come before speed of implementation.

## 2. Corporate positioning

Spotlight Marketing is a comprehensive, technology-driven marketing and business growth agency. It must **not** be positioned primarily as a web development, advertising, social media or door-to-door sales organization.

Individual services are parts of one strategic capability: helping businesses strengthen their market position, generate sustainable revenue opportunities, improve operational effectiveness, and decide well using meaningful performance intelligence.

**Disciplines to communicate.** These are positioning and discovery areas. They are not authorized service offerings.

- Marketing strategy and execution
- Customer acquisition and retention
- Revenue growth and optimization
- Digital infrastructure
- Website design and development
- Paid media and advertising
- Search engine optimization
- Brand strategy and positioning
- Social presence and brand management
- Consumer psychology and behavior
- Conversion rate optimization
- Competitive and market intelligence
- Analytics, attribution and performance reporting
- Business automation and technology integration

**Positioning rules (Approved):**

1. Lead with business outcomes, then service deliverables.
2. No single service may dominate the corporate identity.
3. Listing a discipline does not authorize a commercial offering, a price, a guarantee or a contractual commitment. Those belong to the Founder ([governance §1](01-governance.md#1-authority-structure)).

## 3. Foundational business principles (Approved)

1. Sustainable growth over temporary performance.
2. Measurable business outcomes over vanity metrics.
3. Strategic intelligence over generic execution.
4. Transparent reporting over ambiguous performance claims.
5. Continuous improvement over static service delivery.
6. Client value over unnecessary service expansion.
7. Professional accountability across every engagement.
8. Evidence-based decisions over unsupported assumptions.
9. Long-term relationships over transactional engagements.
10. Premium execution across every client-facing touchpoint.

## 4. Creative direction principles (Approved; nothing is finalized yet)

Aim to combine the strategic sophistication of a leading global consulting firm, the innovation of a world-class technology company and the craft of an exceptional digital design studio. The experience should be bespoke, intentional and commercially effective.

**Avoid:** generic AI-generated aesthetics, template-driven repetition, excessive gradients or glassmorphism, decorative animation without purpose, generic stock imagery, unsupported claims, repetitive card layouts, needless interaction complexity, effects that compromise performance, and trends without strategic purpose.

Typography, color, logos, slogans, imagery, page layouts and visual identity are **not decided** before Phase 03. Each requires strategic discovery and executive review.

Decorative complexity must never cost usability, accessibility, conversion effectiveness or performance.

## 5. Content integrity rules

**Status:** Approved. These rules apply to all content in every phase, including drafts and placeholders.

1. **No fabrication:** no case studies, testimonials, revenue figures, performance statistics, partnerships, certifications, client relationships or capabilities that are not real.
2. **No guarantees** of financial or marketing outcomes.
3. Any claim of fact about the company needs a named source the Founder has confirmed (Phase 01 proof inventory).
4. Placeholder or sample content must be visibly labelled as such. It must never be publishable as-is.
5. Client names, logos and results need documented client permission before use.

## 6. Success definition (Proposed)

Phase 02 (business metrics) and Phase 04 (technical metrics) will formalize this. The working proposal is: qualified opportunity generation measured against a documented baseline, plus verified compliance with the quality standards. Targets will be set from real Phase 01 baselines, not chosen in advance. See [OPEN-012](../registers/open-items.md#open-012).
