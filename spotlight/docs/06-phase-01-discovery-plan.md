# 06 — Phase 01 Strategic Discovery Plan

**Status: Proposed, not authorized.** No work in this plan may start until the Founder issues AUTH-002 ([draft](../registers/authorizations.md#auth-002-proposed)).

## 1. Purpose

Build the evidence base that Phases 02–04 will design from. Every positioning, content, conversion and technical decision later on should trace back to a finding here.

## 2. Key questions

1. **Business:** What does Spotlight actually sell today and to whom? What is it able to deliver (verified capabilities)? Where should it be in 3–5 years? What counts as a *qualified* opportunity?
2. **Audience:** Who buys (company size, sector, geography, decision-maker role)? What triggers their search? What objections and risks worry them? How do they evaluate agencies?
3. **Market:** Who do these buyers compare Spotlight with? How do competitors and best-in-class firms position themselves, prove their claims and convert visitors? Where is the open ground?
4. **Proof:** What real evidence is there (results, clients willing to be named, credentials, team expertise), and what permissions exist to use it?
5. **Current state:** What does the existing website do well and badly, what traffic or search equity does it hold, and what must be kept at migration (URLs, rankings, inbound links)?
6. **Search:** What do buyers search for? How competitive is that? What topical authority is realistic?
7. **Measurement:** What analytics and CRM data exist? Which baselines can targets be set from?

## 3. Inputs required from the Founder (blocking)

- A completed intake questionnaire covering services, clients, pricing posture, geography, growth goals, capacity, differentiators and constraints.
- A list of real proof assets, with what permission each has.
- The current website's URL and platform, and who controls it ([OPEN-005](../registers/open-items.md#open-005)).
- The competitors the Founder already has in mind, and reference sites they admire or dislike.
- A decision on whether agents may access existing analytics or Search Console data. This needs credentials and a separate scope ([OPEN-014](../registers/open-items.md#open-014)).

## 4. Workstreams

| WS | Workstream | Method | Output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Founder and business intake | Structured questionnaire plus a follow-up clarification round | Business profile; verified capabilities list; *qualified lead* definition (draft) |
| B | Existing website audit | Read-only public review: content inventory, IA, messaging, technical/SEO crawl of public pages, accessibility and performance spot checks (lab, labelled as such) | Audit report; URL inventory for future redirects |
| C | Competitive intelligence | 8–12 firms in four groups: direct regional competitors, national growth agencies, consulting-firm benchmarks, digital-experience benchmarks | Competitor matrix (positioning, offers, proof, conversion paths, content depth, technical quality) and a differentiation map |
| D | Audience and buyer journey | Desk research plus Workstream A input; hypotheses explicitly labelled as hypotheses | Audience hypotheses, a journey map, an objection inventory |
| E | Search landscape | Analysis of public search results; keyword volume tools only if authorized | Topic and keyword landscape, intent map, opportunity assessment |
| F | Proof and content inventory | Review of the assets the Founder supplies | Proof inventory, each item marked verified or unverified, with its permission status |

**Sequencing:** A runs first, because the others depend on its scope. B, C, E and F can then run in parallel, with each workstream on its own branch or file. D runs last, after A and C. One coordinator owns synthesis, STATUS.md and the registers.

## 5. Constraints

- Public sources only, unless something else is authorized. Cite every external finding with a URL and access date.
- Do not contact competitors or prospects, submit forms, create accounts, subscribe or download gated content.
- Respect robots.txt and site terms. Use polite, low-volume crawling of the existing site only.
- No paid tools or data without authorization.
- No creative output: no names, slogans or visual explorations.
- Competitor material is for analysis only. Do not copy it into Spotlight content.

## 6. Acceptance criteria

1. Every key question in §2 is answered or explicitly marked unanswerable, with the reason.
2. Findings are labelled *Finding*, *Assumption* or *Hypothesis* and sourced.
3. The competitor matrix covers at least 8 firms across the four groups, using consistent criteria.
4. The existing-site URL inventory is complete for publicly crawlable pages.
5. The proof inventory contains only Founder-confirmed items.
6. A Phase 02 recommendations section exists, with proposed decisions listed separately from approved ones.
7. Delivered through a PR, together with a handoff. Founder acceptance is recorded.

## 7. Deliverables

`spotlight/discovery/` containing: `01-business-profile.md`, `02-existing-site-audit.md`, `03-competitor-matrix.md`, `04-audience-hypotheses.md`, `05-search-landscape.md`, `06-proof-inventory.md`, and `00-discovery-report.md` (the synthesis and Phase 02 recommendations).

## 8. Effort and resourcing (Proposed)

- One coordinating session, plus up to three parallel research agents for B, C and E after A is complete.
- Estimated as 2–4 working sessions of agent time, plus however long the Founder needs for intake. The estimate is unverified. It is re-estimated after A.
